# P10 demo shot list

This script is designed to fit in 60 seconds. Keep the browser on the live URL and show the product working; architecture belongs in the README, not the recording.

1. **0:00 to 0:10.** Start on PUB-01. Say: "KotoDin rebuilds a prepaid meter one day at a time. This official case has six months, 181 daily readings, a light January, a heavy May, and a ৳4,300 recharge in May's last week."
2. **0:10 to 0:23.** Point to the hero. Say: "After every recharge, slab charge, VAT and monthly fixed charge, today's balance is ৳2,080.84. At 19 units a day it runs out on 20 July."
3. **0:23 to 0:37.** Scroll to the ledger and drag the day scrubber onto a recharge. Say: "The line marks every recharge. I can audit any date, and the monthly table separates deposits, energy, VAT, fixed charges and closing balance."
4. **0:37 to 0:51.** Scroll to the target-date calculator and change the date. Say: "To last through a chosen date, the top-up recalculates immediately. The answer reconciles base energy, the higher-slab uplift, fixed charges, VAT and the balance already available."
5. **0:50 to 1:00.** Show the habit comparison. Say: "Both habits use identical consumption and the same slab counter. PUB-01 correctly shows equal consumed cost. Deposits differ, but recharge timing creates no fake energy saving."

Before recording, use a desktop window wide enough to show both sides of each comparison. Do one practice run and keep the final video at or under 60 seconds.
